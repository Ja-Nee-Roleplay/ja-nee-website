# Design concept

Corporate Professional was selected through the original randomized style draw, then evolved into a modern, community-first direction for Ja-Nee RP. The first impression is a confident, open green canvas in #165209, with deep evergreen panels and measured gold accents that make the calls to action and live status easy to spot. Rounded surfaces, clear type hierarchy, and a generous hero give the page the calm, polished feel of an established community.

STIX Two Text gives the headings an editorial voice, while Montserrat ExtraBold adds energy to the short supporting lines and key labels. Spacing, split compositions, tactile rounded controls, and restrained transitions keep each section distinct while helping visitors scan and move through the page. The experience begins with an invitation to explore Ja-Nee RP, builds context through community and practical details, and finishes with a clear Discord joining point.

The visual references combine contemporary editorial layouts, civic wayfinding, and clean community platforms such as Discord: strong modular rhythm, simple navigation, and content blocks that feel easy to approach. Forest greens and warm metallic tones connect those references to Ja-Nee RP’s own identity, while the illustrations and details remain original rather than reproducing another brand’s assets or visual composition.
