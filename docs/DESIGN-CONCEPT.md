# Design concept

Corporate Professional was selected through the original randomized style draw, then refined into a welcoming dark editorial direction for Ja-Nee RP. Forest green (#165209) and warm gold anchor the identity against a deep evergreen canvas. Lighter translucent panels, readable ivory text, and the city illustration keep the interface calm and clear without switching into light-mode sections.

STIX Two Text gives the headings a distinctive editorial voice; Montserrat ExtraBold brings clarity to short supporting lines, controls, and labels. Rounded surfaces use restrained glass blur and soft shadows to sit above the dark canvas, while a persistent chapter guide and gentle scroll stops make movement feel deliberate without trapping visitors. The experience opens with an invitation, builds context through community and practical guidance, then closes on a quiet, clearly signposted community connection.

The visual research draws on the confident spacing and modular rhythm of [Linear](https://linear.app/), the friendly hierarchy of [Discord](https://discord.com/), and the clear onboarding flow of [The Hideout](https://thehideout.gg/). Those references inform the page’s calm navigation, distinct content chapters, and approachable calls to action; all Ja-Nee RP artwork and interface details remain original. Unconfigured community links and live server data stay clearly identified instead of being replaced with invented information.
